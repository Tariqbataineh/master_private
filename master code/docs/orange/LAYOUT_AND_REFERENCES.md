Right corridor travels positive X, with classroom then coaches on its negative-Z side (traveller's left); manager is the terminal room. Services corridor travels negative X; kitchen is negative Z (traveller's right); both restrooms are positive Z (traveller's left). Language never transforms world geometry.

## Estimates and limits
This is a conceptual reconstruction, not a surveyed digital twin. Internal model units, room sizes, corridor widths, door widths, hidden connections and additional room separation are visual estimates supporting the authoritative written arrangement. Footage does not securely identify the two offices; visible office furniture informs both. Kitchen cabinetry, sink, kettle and fridge; restroom fixtures, partitions and accessible grab rails are requested inferred elements. Ramp alongside shallow steps is an estimated presentation route, not evidence of a real accessible entrance. No compliance certification or exact dimensions are claimed. Generic student/coach figures and sanitized programming displays are invented for this presentation. No footage faces, documents or screen captures are shipped as textures.
